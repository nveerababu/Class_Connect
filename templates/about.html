About ClassConnect

ClassConnect is a web-based Student Management System developed to keep student records organized and easily accessible. It replaces manual paperwork with a simple digital platform to manage enrollments, track student progress, and monitor course details.

Student Records

Perform basic CRUD operations to add, view, update, and remove student information easily.

Course Tracking

Organize students based on their enrolled courses along with duration and fee structures.

Status Updates

Monitor whether a student is currently Active, Enrolled, or has Completed their course.

Technologies Used

  • Backend: Python & Django Framework
  • Database: SQLite
  • Frontend: HTML5 & CSS3
